## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Don't Blush is primarily a first-person interactive FMV game rather than a traditional free-roaming action game. Progression is controlled through scene transitions, dialogue choices, investigations, and supernatural interactions. Use chapter access and scene replay features only after the relevant game content has loaded.

### Tools & Inventory

The game uses supernatural tools, talismans, learned spells, and related story resources rather than a conventional equipment-heavy inventory. Spell and progression options are generally tied to story advancement, so activate unlock features from a stable gameplay scene and allow the game to save before continuing.

### World & Environment

The story moves between ordinary campus-life settings and a hidden supernatural world. Exploration and investigation use choices, clues, the compass, the five elements, special abilities, and alternate interactions. Progress through scenes normally before forcing route or chapter changes.

### Combat & Weapons

Supernatural encounters include talisman-drawing sequences, spell use, defensive choices, and action or timing events. Combat-related trainer options are intended to assist with these sequences, not to turn the game into a conventional real-time combat system.
